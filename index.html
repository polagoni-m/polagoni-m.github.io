About the work.

OPERATOR · PROFILE

I work where perception meets control — where a model has to look at the world and act on it before the next frame lands.

My research at Northeastern combines classical robotics — Kalman filtering, Hungarian assignment, sampling-based MPC, sensor fusion — with modern learning-based approaches like deep reinforcement learning, vision transformers, and Gaussian process planners. I care about systems that hold up outside benchmarks: latency budgets, occlusion, domain shift, the unglamorous failure modes.

Recent projects span SLAM on a snake-inspired robot (RTAB-Map onboard COBRA), multi-object tracking from scratch (YOLOv8 + custom Kalman + Hungarian, 5.9–8.9 FPS), deep RL for autonomous racing (PPO vs SAC vs TD3 on F1TENTH, with SAC delivering 2× the reward of classical baselines), and GP-guided MPPI navigation in unknown cluttered environments.

Before grad school I worked as a research intern at the Defence Research and Development Laboratory (DRDL) in Hyderabad on actuator control. Outside the lab: Formula 1, Marvel marathons, exploring Boston.
