About the work.

I work on the parts of robotic systems that have to be right in real time — perception, state estimation, and the geometry that ties pixels and sensor readings back to the physical world.

My research at Northeastern combines classical robotics — Kalman filtering, Hungarian assignment, MPC, sensor fusion — with modern learning-based approaches like deep RL, vision transformers, and Gaussian process planners. I'm drawn to problems where perception meets control, where a model has to look at the world and act on it before the next frame.

Recent projects span SLAM on snake-like robots (RTAB-Map onboard COBRA), multi-object tracking from scratch (YOLOv8 + custom Kalman + Hungarian), deep RL for autonomous racing (PPO vs SAC vs TD3 on F1TENTH), and GP-guided MPPI navigation in unknown cluttered environments.

Before grad school, I worked as a research intern at the Defence Research and Development Laboratory (DRDL) in Hyderabad on DC motor control. Outside the lab: Formula 1, Marvel marathons, and exploring Boston.

EECE 5550 · Mobile Robotics Final · Spring 2025

GP-guided MPPI for Navigation in Unknown Cluttered Environments

Implementation of a Gaussian Process-guided Model Predictive Path Integral controller, following Mohamed et al. (2023). A Sparse Gaussian Process occupancy model built from LiDAR estimates both occupancy and uncertainty — high-variance regions become subgoals that guide MPPI beyond its planning horizon, escaping local-minima traps that hinder vanilla MPPI in cluttered maps. Evaluated across three environments of escalating difficulty.

CS 6983 · HRI Final · Spring 2026

Evaluating HRI with a Competitive Cozmo Robot

with R. A. Fernandes, L. Chandrasekar, K. Vellanki — Team Velocity
A within-subjects user study (N=24) evaluating how robot personality modulates user perception during a synchronous Rock-Paper-Scissors game with an Anki Cozmo. Built a custom Python pipeline using MediaPipe Hands (21 3D landmarks) on a laptop webcam after Cozmo's onboard camera proved too laggy for real-time gesture recognition. Three conditions — Neutral, Supportive, Competitive — modulated verbal, facial, and physical reactions. Supportive rated friendliest, but Competitive drove the highest voluntary engagement.

GPS & IMU Sensor Fusion for Automotive Dead Reckoning

Muscle-Driven Simulation of Human Stair Gait in OpenSim

with Deelip Kumar Gummadidhala
End-to-end pipeline for muscle-driven simulation of stair ambulation using OpenSim 4.5 and MATLAB. Processed raw kinematic and kinetic data from the Darmstadt Stair Ambulation Dataset — coordinate transforms, temporal sync, and signal smoothing. Validated muscle activation patterns against experimental EMG; quantified concentric hip/ankle propulsion in ascent vs. eccentric knee control in descent.

Single-Axis Magnetic Levitation: State-Space Control Design

with A. Mashhadireza, R. Suhail, H. A. Syed
